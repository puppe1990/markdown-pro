# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People writing and organizing Markdown documents in a focused workspace. They need to move quickly between editing, previewing, recovering versions, and exporting without decoding the interface.

## Product Purpose

Markdown Pro provides a dependable writing environment with live preview, document organization, version history, and export. Success means the interface recedes while writing and makes every document action immediately recognizable.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, editorial. Familiar like Apple Notes and Notion, without imitating either product.

## Anti-references

Avoid ornamental productivity dashboards, ambiguous icon-only controls, visually interchangeable actions, and decorative effects that compete with the document.

## Design Principles

- Make writing the dominant visual activity.
- Give each action a distinct, familiar affordance.
- Reveal detail progressively through labels, tooltips, and panels.
- Prefer quiet consistency over novelty.
- Preserve user trust through clear state and recovery cues.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA. Icon-only actions require accessible names, visible keyboard focus, and persistent hover or focus tooltips. State must never rely on color alone, and reduced-motion preferences must be respected.
